# Authoritative source ecosystems

Starting points for research, not a dump of URLs and not a permission slip to scrape everything. Prefer the current official page. Verify before citing. Create a YAML record only for a work you will actually use.

Discovery blogs and social posts can still *lead* here. They should not *replace* these layers for technical or safety claims. See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) and [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md).

## How to use this list

1. Identify the claim type (safety, chemistry, history, producer, sensory).
2. Open the matching ecosystem below.
3. Follow citations outward to the specific paper, method, regulation, or finding aid.
4. Record locator and `verified` date on the source YAML.

## Baking, grain, flour, bread science

| Layer | Why it matters | Start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cereal science society | Methods, definitions, journal literature | [Cereals & Grains Association](https://cerealsgrains.org/) (formerly AACC International); Approved Methods of Analysis |
| University grain / bakery science | Teaching labs, milling and bakery degrees, applied research | [K-State Grain and Food Science](https://www.grains.k-state.edu/) including [Bakery Science & Management](https://www.grains.k-state.edu/academics/majors/bakery-science-management/) |
| U.S. flour identity | Legal names of flours, not marketing names | [21 CFR Part 137](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-137) (already in the corpus as `gg:source:ecfr-21-cfr-137`) |
| Analytical chemistry methods | Official methods beyond cereal | [AOAC International](https://www.aoac.org/) |
| Professional craft guild | Practitioner education, not a substitute for cereal chemistry | [Bread Bakers Guild of America](https://bbga.org/) |
| Technical bakery books | Dough temperature, mixing, fermentation as practiced | Calvel, *The Taste of Bread* (`gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread`); also look up Hamelman, Suas, Gisslen in a library — cite the edition in hand |
| Flour technical sheets | Ash, protein, falling number, absorption as *that mill’s* specs | Producer or mill PDF; treat as claims about that product |
| Food chemistry / microbiology | Mechanisms that baking applies | University food-science departments; peer-reviewed fermentation and cereal journals |
| USDA / FDA | When the claim is labeling, identity, or safety, not “artisan tips” | [FDA Foods](https://www.fda.gov/food); [USDA](https://www.usda.gov/) |

King Arthur Baking’s technical blog is **quality secondary / practitioner** writing. Useful for DDT pedagogy; not the top of the pyramid for cereal chemistry.

## Preserving and home food safety

| Layer | Start |
| --- | --- |
| Tested home preservation | [National Center for Home Food Preservation](https://nchfp.uga.edu/) (University of Georgia; USDA-linked research and recommendations) |
| USDA canning guide | NCHFP’s [USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning](https://nchfp.uga.edu/resources/category/usda-guide) collection |
| Extension | State Cooperative Extension (search the relevant state + “home canning”); UGA *So Easy to Preserve* |
| Federal food safety | [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food); [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/) for meat and poultry products |

Do not substitute hobby canning blogs for process times.

## Wine

| Layer | Start |
| --- | --- |
| Viticulture and enology | [UC Davis Department of Viticulture and Enology](https://wineserver.ucdavis.edu/) (confirmed as the departmental site; some automated fetches are bot-blocked). Stable catalog entry: [Viticulture & Enology](https://catalog.ucdavis.edu/departments-programs-degrees/viticulture-enology/). College page: [CAES](https://caes.ucdavis.edu/research/dept/viti) |
| U.S. appellations | [TTB Wine Appellations of Origin](https://www.ttb.gov/appellations-of-origin); [established AVAs](https://www.ttb.gov/node/1267); 27 CFR part 9 for official boundaries |
| Spatial / historical AVA work | [UC Davis AVA Digitizing Project](https://ucdavislibrary.github.io/ava/) as a research aid; TTB remains the regulator |
| Other university programs | Cornell, Fresno State, and comparable enology programs for teaching and extension |
| Producer claims | The winery’s TTB-compliant label and current site |

*Terroir* is an interpretive idea with scientific and cultural layers. Do not treat a tasting note as an AVA regulation.

## San Francisco / Bay Area food history

| Layer | Start |
| --- | --- |
| City archives | [SFPL San Francisco History Center](https://sfpl.org/locations/main-library/sf-history-center) |
| Statewide finding aids | [Online Archive of California](https://oac.cdlib.org/) |
| Historical society collections | California Historical Society holdings transferred to **Stanford University Libraries** (2025); cite the current finding aid, e.g. menu collections on OAC |
| Museums | Local museum catalogs for objects and ephemera; do not invent accessions |
| Producer histories | Official bakery/creamery/winery history pages, then corroborate dates |
| Regional journalism | Named outlets with dates; prefer reporting over listicles |

Sourdough’s San Francisco reputation needs this layer plus microbiology — not a slogan.

## Cross-cutting scientific literature

- University library search, Google Scholar, or publisher sites for **the paper**, then cite DOI
- Extension publications for applied food safety
- Codex / ISO / national standards when the claim is a standard of identity or method

## What not to treat as an ecosystem

Generic recipe aggregators, unsourced commercial “ultimate guides,” forum consensus, and AI-written roundups. They are discovery at best.
