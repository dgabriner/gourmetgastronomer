# Vision

Gourmet Gastronomer is a digital food academy, encyclopedia, practical skills library, industry reference, and San Francisco / Bay Area food atlas.

It should make the world of food more interesting, understandable, navigable, and delicious. Readers become better cooks, bakers, tasters, shoppers, diners, travelers, preservers, fermenters, gardeners, students, and participants in their local food economy.

The site rewards curiosity. A starter article should be able to lead to wild yeast, lactic acid bacteria, fermentation, flour, wheat, mills, bakeries, ovens, bread history, cheese, wine, and local producers — without copying the same explanation onto every page.

## Lineage

Gourmet Gastronomer predates, and conceptually gave rise to, Sour Flour. Sour Flour became a real-world expression of one portion of this idea: learning food deeply, mastering a craft, teaching others, and building community around food.

This site is not a Sour Flour marketing site. The lineage is:

**Gourmet Gastronomer → exploration and food education → deep bread knowledge → Sour Flour**

Lineage is recorded on About and `/sour-flour/`. The site uses a bounded knowledge-to-practice bridge on the homepage, in the footer, and on a small number of bread pages where the next action is genuinely useful. It is not a storefront and not a global-nav item.

## Non-goals

User accounts, comments, personalization engines, a CMS as source of truth, e-commerce, and mass-generated empty pages.
