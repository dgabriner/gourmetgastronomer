Gluten is not an ingredient you add. It forms from proteins already in [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]] once [[gg:ingredient:water]] and work are present.

[[gg:topic:mixing]] and folds are ways of organizing that network. Too little work and the dough cannot hold gas; too much and it tears. Calvel treats flour behavior — including gluten — as something the baker learns by dough feel as well as by specification [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

See [[gg:topic:dough-development]] for the craft application.
