This is a teaching formula for practicing the sourdough sequence on this site. It is not a published bakery recipe and it is not [[gg:place:tartine-bakery]]’s loaf.

Use ripe [[gg:topic:sourdough-starter]], mix with [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]], [[gg:ingredient:water]], and [[gg:ingredient:salt]], then follow [[gg:topic:bulk-fermentation]], [[gg:topic:shaping]], [[gg:topic:final-proof]], [[gg:topic:scoring]], and [[gg:topic:bake]]. Calvel remains a technical companion for flour and fermentation behavior, not a source for these exact weights [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

1. Mix flour and most of the water; rest (autolyse) as your schedule allows.
2. Add starter and salt; mix until the dough is cohesive.
3. Bulk ferment with folds until the dough is aerated and strong enough to shape.
4. Divide, shape two bâtards, and proof.
5. Score and bake on a hot stone or in a covered pot until the crust is deep and the loaf feels light.
