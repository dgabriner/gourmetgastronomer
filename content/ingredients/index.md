Ingredient pages live here so bread, cheese, jam, and wine do not each invent their own flour or milk. A baker, a cheesemaker, and a preserving kettle can point at the same [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]] or [[gg:ingredient:milk]]. Fruit belongs here too: [[gg:ingredient:strawberry]] for jam, [[gg:ingredient:grape]] for wine and jelly.

This is a catalog of substances, not a shopping list.
