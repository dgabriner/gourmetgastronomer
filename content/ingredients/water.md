In bread, water is not a neutral backdrop. Hydration changes how [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]] feels, how [[gg:topic:gluten]] develops, and how fast [[gg:topic:fermentation]] runs. Starters are flour plus water before they are anything else.

Mineral content and temperature are real variables. This page does not invent a municipal water profile for San Francisco or anywhere else. Taste the dough; adjust [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].
