The final proof is not a second bulk. The dough is already organized by [[gg:topic:shaping]]; now it needs enough [[gg:topic:fermentation]] to be lively in the oven without collapsing.

Underproofed loaves burst wildly. Overproofed loaves flatten. The poke test is a clue, not a law. Proofing temperature is a lever, as it is throughout breadmaking [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

When the loaf is ready, [[gg:topic:scoring]] opens a path for expansion and [[gg:topic:bake|baking]] sets the structure.
