Levain is starter given a job. You take some [[gg:topic:sourdough-starter]], add [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]] and water, and ferment that mixture until it is ripe for the dough you intend to mix.

The point is control: inoculation, acidity, and gas production can be aimed at a bake time rather than at whatever state the storage starter happens to be in. That control is part of professional breadmaking as Calvel describes it [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

Ripe levain then enters [[gg:topic:mixing]] and continues its work in [[gg:topic:bulk-fermentation]].
