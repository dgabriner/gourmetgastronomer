A starter is not a recipe. It is a population. Flour and [[gg:ingredient:water]] are the habitat; [[gg:topic:wild-yeast]] and [[gg:topic:lactic-acid-bacteria]] are the residents.

Feeding (refreshing) the starter is a way of selecting for microbes that raise dough rather than merely souring it. The flour you feed matters: [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]] brings enzymes and a particular mineral and protein profile [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

When a portion of starter is built into a larger, timed preferment for a specific dough, bakers often call that a [[gg:topic:levain]]. The two words overlap in home kitchens; the distinction is useful in a bakery.
