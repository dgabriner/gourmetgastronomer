Most sourdough problems are not mysteries. They are mismatches among [[gg:topic:sourdough-starter]] ripeness, [[gg:topic:bulk-fermentation]], dough strength, and [[gg:topic:bake|the bake]].

A dense crumb often means underfermentation or weak [[gg:topic:gluten]]. A loaf that spreads may have been shaped without tension or proofed too far. Harsh acidity can mean a starter kept too warm, too hungry, or both. None of these diagnoses replace looking at the dough [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].
