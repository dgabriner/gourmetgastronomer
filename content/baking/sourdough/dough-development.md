Dough development is the felt difference between a shaggy mass and a dough that can hold gas. [[gg:topic:gluten]] is the protein network; time, water, and mechanical work are how it organizes.

Folds during [[gg:topic:bulk-fermentation]] are a gentle way to continue the work that [[gg:topic:mixing]] started. Flour strength varies: [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]] is not one ingredient [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

Underdeveloped dough slumps. Overdeveloped dough tears. The baker learns the middle by touch.
