Shaping takes a piece of bulk-fermented dough and gives it a skin and a direction. The baker degasses only as much as the style requires, then tightens the surface without tearing the [[gg:topic:gluten]] network built during [[gg:topic:dough-development]].

A well-shaped loaf sits with tension. A poorly shaped loaf spreads. That tension is what [[gg:topic:scoring]] later exploits.

This is handcraft more than chemistry, but it depends on the fermentation that came before [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread]. After shaping comes the [[gg:topic:final-proof]].
