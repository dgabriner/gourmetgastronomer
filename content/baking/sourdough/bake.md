Baking is not a single temperature. Steam, stone or steel, and oven spring in the first minutes decide the crust; dry heat afterward finishes the crumb.

The loaf that enters the oven is the sum of [[gg:topic:fermentation]] and [[gg:topic:dough-development]]. Heat does not correct a dough that was never ready. Calvel’s technical writing treats the oven as the last of several linked operations [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

A teaching formula for practicing this sequence is [[gg:recipe:country-loaf]]. In San Francisco, [[gg:place:tartine-bakery]] is one public place where hearth bread is baked every day.
