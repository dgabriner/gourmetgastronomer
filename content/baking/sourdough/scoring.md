Scoring is a controlled wound. Steam and heat inflate the loaf; the cut is where the baker wants that expansion to go.

The angle, depth, and timing of the cut depend on how the [[gg:topic:final-proof]] went and on how much surface tension [[gg:topic:shaping]] left behind. A loaf that is not ready to bake will not suddenly become ready because it was cut well.
