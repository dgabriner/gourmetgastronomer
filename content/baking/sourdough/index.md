Sourdough is a way of raising dough with a living culture — flour and water that have captured [[gg:topic:wild-yeast]] and [[gg:topic:lactic-acid-bacteria]] — instead of packaged baker’s yeast.

The culture (the [[gg:topic:sourdough-starter|starter]]) inoculates the dough. During [[gg:topic:fermentation]] the microbes make gas, acids, and aroma. Flour choice matters: protein, ash, and enzyme activity in [[gg:ingredient:wheat-flour]] change how that fermentation feels [src:gg:source:calvel-taste-of-bread].

San Francisco’s reputation for this bread is real history and real microbiology, and it is also a magnet for folklore. That argument has its own page: [[gg:topic:san-francisco-sourdough]]. A working bakery in the city is [[gg:place:tartine-bakery]]. For a teaching loaf with baker’s percentages, see [[gg:recipe:country-loaf]].
