Wine is [[gg:ingredient:grape]] juice that [[gg:topic:fermentation]] has taken past must. Yeast converts much of the sugar to ethanol and carbon dioxide. What remains — acid, residual sugar, phenolics, aroma compounds — is the drink. This page is the craft skeleton. Sensory method lives on [[gg:topic:wine-tasting]]. Shared biology lives on the fermentation page; do not expect a second copy of microbial metabolism here.

## Must, sugar, and alcoholic fermentation

Crushed grapes are must: juice, skins, seeds, and whatever pulp the press or punch-down keeps in contact. White wines often separate juice from skins early. Red wines keep skins (and often seeds) in the ferment so color and [[gg:topic:tannin]] extract. That contact is maceration — a time-and-temperature extraction, not a synonym for jam-making maceration, though both words mean “let sit in liquid.”

Alcoholic fermentation is yeast on grape sugar. It is not sourdough’s mixed LAB-and-yeast paste, and it is not cheese cultures on lactose. Same word, different habitat. Inoculated *Saccharomyces* and spontaneous ferments are both used; this encyclopedia will not pretend they are interchangeable recipes.

## Structure, not poetry

A useful tasting grammar is physical:

- **[[gg:topic:acidity]]** — tartaric and other acids keep wine from tasting flat and change how tannin feels.
- **[[gg:topic:tannin]]** — phenolics that dry the mouth by binding saliva protein [src:gg:source:smith-tannin-ajgw-2015].
- **Alcohol** — weight and warmth; a fermentation product, not a garnish.
- **Sugar** — residual sweetness if fermentation stops or is stopped.
- **Aroma** — grape, fermentation esters, and later oak or bottle age.

## Place is a label rule, then a climate

In the U.S., an appellation of origin can be a country, state, county, or a delimited viticultural area (AVA) [src:gg:source:ttb-appellations]. An AVA tells you where grapes were grown under TTB rules. It does not mandate a grape variety or a cellar method. Northern California’s teaching geographies in this atlas are [[gg:place:napa-valley]] and [[gg:place:sonoma-county]] — fog, hills, and San Pablo Bay as cooling, not a winery directory.

[[gg:place:san-francisco]] drinks wine. It is not, by itself, a vineyard. Aging vessels and oxygen are [[gg:topic:wine-aging]].
