Work these pages in order the first time. FAO’s four stages — coagulate, drain, salt, ripen — are the spine [src:gg:source:fao-small-scale-cheese]. [[gg:topic:cheese]] is the hub, not a step you must reread between every other page.

After the path, wander: [[gg:topic:cheese-rind]] from affinage; [[gg:place:cowgirl-creamery]] or [[gg:place:point-reyes-farmstead]] for North Bay milk. The sequence is a lesson, not a cage.
