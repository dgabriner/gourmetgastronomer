The Ferry Building stands at the foot of Market Street. The marketplace’s visitor page gives the address as One Ferry Building, San Francisco, California 94111 [src:gg:source:ferry-building-visit]. The official site describes a hall of local food merchants in a landmark the marketplace dates to 1898 [src:gg:source:ferry-building-marketplace].

This is an atlas node for a market hall, not a complete tenant list. [[gg:place:acme-bread]] bakes here as the hall’s “village baker,” by the bakery’s own account of 2002 [src:gg:source:acme-bread-about]. [[gg:place:cowgirl-creamery]] once had a shop here; that shop closed in 2021. Fruit such as [[gg:ingredient:strawberry]] is why a city market matters to [[gg:topic:jam]]. Individual merchants should receive their own place pages when they are sourced.

Hours of the building and of each stall change; they are not recorded here. Use the marketplace site.
