Cowgirl Creamery is in this atlas as a cheesemaker, not as a current San Francisco counter. The company’s site presents the work as North Bay / Sonoma County cheesemaking [src:gg:source:cowgirl-creamery-site].

Cowgirl published its own farewell to the Ferry Building shop in 2021 [src:gg:source:cowgirl-ferry-building-2021]. That history still connects the creamery to [[gg:place:ferry-building]] and to [[gg:place:san-francisco]], but it is history. [[gg:place:sonoma-county]] is the living geography. [[gg:place:point-reyes-farmstead]] is a neighboring coastal dairy-and-creamery, not a rival listing.

Follow [[gg:topic:cheese]], [[gg:ingredient:milk]], and [[gg:topic:affinage]] for the craft. Do not treat this page as a store locator.
