Point Reyes Farmstead is a dairy story that grew a cheese make, not a shop that rented a farm aesthetic. The company’s history page places the family in Point Reyes farming since Waldo Giacomini’s move in 1938; Bob and Dean Giacomini bought 710 acres overlooking Tomales Bay in 1959 and shipped milk [src:gg:source:point-reyes-cheese-about]. In the 1990s the herd was larger than the land and labor could sustain as a commodity dairy. In August 2000 the daughters launched the creamery with Original Blue, which the company calls California’s only classic-style blue [src:gg:source:point-reyes-cheese-about]. Treat that “only” as a producer claim about style, not a census this encyclopedia has independently audited.

Original Blue is still made at the Point Reyes farm. In 2018 a second creamery, aging rooms, packaging, and distribution opened in Petaluma, about 22 miles east in [[gg:place:sonoma-county]] [src:gg:source:point-reyes-cheese-about]. Headquarters remain 14700 State Highway 1, Point Reyes Station [src:gg:source:point-reyes-cheese-contact]. Hours and tour tickets are not recorded here.
