The atlas is not a directory of hours. It is geography attached to knowledge. A bakery can point at [[gg:topic:sourdough]]; a creamery can point at [[gg:topic:cheese]]; a market hall can point at both.

Start with [[gg:place:san-francisco]]. City bakeries include [[gg:place:tartine-bakery]], [[gg:place:boudin-bakery]], and [[gg:place:arizmendi-bakery]]. East Bay hearth bread: [[gg:place:acme-bread]]. A Peninsula mill and grain processor: [[gg:place:giustos]]. A market hall: [[gg:place:ferry-building]]. North Bay dairy: [[gg:place:cowgirl-creamery]], [[gg:place:point-reyes-farmstead]], [[gg:place:marin-county]]. Grape-growing geographies, not winery directories: [[gg:place:napa-valley]] and [[gg:place:sonoma-county]]. Cheese and bread in one Berkeley shop: [[gg:place:cheese-board-collective]].
