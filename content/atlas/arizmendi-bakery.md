Arizmendi on 9th Avenue is a neighborhood bakery whose ownership structure is the food story. The shop’s site identifies a worker-owned cooperative at 1331 9th Avenue, between Irving and Judah, specializing in morning pastries, artisan breads, and pizza, two blocks from Golden Gate Park [src:gg:source:arizmendi-bakery-sf]. It lists sister cooperatives that are independently operated — including The Cheeseboard in Berkeley — with different hours and products [src:gg:source:arizmendi-bakery-sf]. Hours are omitted here on purpose.

[[gg:place:cheese-board-collective]] describes itself as a worker-owned co-op since 1971, with a cheese shop that became a bakery and pizzeria [src:gg:source:cheese-board-about]. Arizmendi bakeries are the spread of that cooperative baking model across the Bay Area, not a chain with a single owner. The name points at José María Arizmendiarrieta and Mondragon; this page does not retell Basque industrial history.

Why it is in the atlas: Inner Sunset bread and pizza as daily food; worker ownership as an economic fact of Bay Area baking, beside founder-led shops such as [[gg:place:tartine-bakery]]. The loaf craft is still [[gg:topic:sourdough]] and [[gg:topic:baking]]. This is not a ranking.
