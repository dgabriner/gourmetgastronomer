Tasting is a skill, not a gift. Slow down. Notice temperature, texture, and what happens after you swallow. Then try to name only what you actually perceived. Vocabulary is a tool when it points at a sensation; it is noise when it performs expertise.

The same attention applies to a [[gg:topic:sourdough]] crumb, a piece of cheese, a glass of wine, and a ripe [[gg:ingredient:strawberry]]. Craft sequences live next door so this page stays general: [[gg:topic:wine-tasting]] for a glass, [[gg:topic:cheese-tasting]] for paste and rind. [[gg:topic:acidity]] is one shared sensation with very different jobs in dough, jam, wine, and canning.
